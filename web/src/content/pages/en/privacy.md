## What stays with you

## What your browser stores

To remember your choices between visits, the site saves the following in your browser's local storage:

- the theme (light or dark, and the game theme, like Fire or Leaf) and the language;
- from the RNG page and the editor, your game (version, console, TID, SID, OT name and the OPTION menu combination) and the timer settings (beeps, sound, flash and adjustments); the chosen seed and target only last while the page is open;
- how far your console's seed lands above or below the table, from the checks you confirmed, used to adjust the seed list;
{{#analytics}}
- whether you accepted or declined the statistics cookies;
{{/analytics}}
- which boxes you've marked as typed on the Codes page;
- the last site version whose news you've seen, so the what's new notice doesn't show again;
- how far you got in each guide with Professor Oak, so you can continue where you left off.

This never leaves your device. To erase it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

{{#analytics}}
## Visit statistics (Google Analytics)

With your permission, in the cookie banner, the site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used the most.

- It only loads after you click Accept. If you decline or ignore the banner, nothing from Google is downloaded.
- It stores cookies (`_ga`) and sends Google the page visited, the browser, the device type and the approximate region. Google Analytics 4 doesn't store your IP address.
- Nothing you do in the tools (files, Pokémon, seeds, box names) is sent.
- To change your choice, use the Cookies link in the footer. Declining after accepting stops sending data and deletes the Google Analytics cookies.

{{/analytics}}
