## Use it on your own game

- Use the tools on your own game and your own console.
- Don't use created or modified Pokémon in competitions, in trades with people who don't know about it, or in online services. Don't take these Pokémon to Pokémon HOME.
- The ESP32 board uses console keys. Only use the ones from your own console. The site does not provide, accept or distribute keys.
- Respect the laws of your country and Nintendo's terms for your devices.
