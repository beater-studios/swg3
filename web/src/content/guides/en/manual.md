The game decides nature, IVs and shininess with a number generator that depends on timing. Hit the right moment and you choose what you get.

1. **Pick the initial seed.** It's set when you press the button on the title screen, the one with PRESS START. The [RNG](/rng) page shows the known Switch seeds and the timing for each one.
2. **Find your target.** Search for the Pokémon you want and write down the frame advance it comes out on.
3. **Time it.** Use the page's timer: it beeps when it's time to press A at PRESS START, at CONTINUE and when it's time to interact with the Pokémon.
4. **Adjust.** Enter what you got. The site figures out which seed and frame advance you hit and corrects the timer for your next attempt.

:::tip Be patient
Missing the first few is normal. Every attempt makes the timer more accurate.
:::
