:::tip Rather do it together?
The [guide with Professor Oak](/editor?tour=editor-create) shows in the editor itself how to create the Pokémon and choose how to take it to the game.
:::

Create or open the Pokémon in [Editor](/editor), make sure it has no errors and open "Put in your game". Pick the way that suits you:

- **Base64 writer.** A single code with the complete Pokémon. Needs the writer installed. [How to install](/guides/base64)
- **Community codes.** One code creates it and others adjust it. Nature, nickname and OT stay however they come out. [Which codes](/guides/codes)
- **ESP32 board.** Your PC trades the Pokémon to you, no typing needed. In progress. [About the board](/guides/esp32)

:::tip Trade evolution
Through the board it's a real trade: a Kadabra will evolve when it arrives. If you don't want that, give it an Everstone to hold.
:::
