## -

Hello there! Community codes are box names: you type them in the game's PC and, with ACE unlocked on your save, the game runs them. Let me show you how to check a code and type it without missing a letter.

## codes-safety

First of all: save the game and keep Box 13 empty from slot 7 on, plus the boxes after it. A wrong code changes the save, and saving first lets you go back.

## codes-input go=codes:example wait=codes:checked

Paste the code's names here, the way they came, and click **Check**. For practice, I left two example names, which in the game are just box names and do nothing: just click **Check**.

## codes-summary

The site checks each letter against the game's table and warns about what the Switch should reject. With everything green, you can type.

## codes-typing

This is the game's box naming screen. Pick the box at the top and click **Next letter**: the cursor moves to the right key, and you do the same in the game. At the end, mark the box as typed and go to the next one. The site remembers where you stopped.

## codes-after

With every box typed, follow these steps to save and trigger ACE, and to check that it worked.

## -

That's it! Remember: always save before triggering, and don't take Pokémon created or changed by codes to Pokémon HOME. Happy coding, trainer!
