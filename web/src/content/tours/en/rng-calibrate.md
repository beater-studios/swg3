title: Calibrate the timer
summary: A beep on PRESS START and two wild Pokémon show how early or late your press lands.
---

## -

Let's calibrate the timer! Your press on PRESS START never lands exactly on the expected instant, and each console has its own offset. Calibrating once, the site measures how early or late you press and corrects the timer by itself. No target needed.

## rng-seed-list go=rng:seed wait=rng:seed-chosen

First, a seed from the list: click **Use** on any of them. It's only the reference point for the check.

## rng-check-tab go=rng:check

Calibration lives in the **Check seed** tab. I already opened it for you.

## rng-check-timer go=rng:check

Save where you'll look for wild Pokémon and close the game. Open the game and click **Start** your usual way. On the title screen, press the button on the last beep, the highest one. Then press space when you enter CONTINUE and again when the battle starts, on the flash.

## rng-check-form go=rng:check

Catch the first Pokémon that shows up and, if you can, the second one too, in the same attempt. Fill in the map, the species, the nature and the stats right after the catch, and click **Find out**. With two Pokémon, the result is almost always right.

## -

Done! When it matches, the site keeps the offset and corrects the timer, and it also learns how far your console's seed lands from the table. Do it again if you change how you start the timer, the console or the game version. Then follow the first seed guide to aim at a real Pokémon.
