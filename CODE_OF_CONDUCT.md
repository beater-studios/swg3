# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SWG3 to be a welcoming place for everyone who plays, researches or builds tools for these games, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- being kind and patient, especially with people new to RNG, ACE or programming;
- respecting different opinions and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- crediting the work of others, in this project and in the community it builds on.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- insults, harassment, or sexualized language or imagery;
- personal or political attacks, trolling;
- publishing someone else's private information without their permission;
- sharing other people's console keys, or tools and payloads meant to be used on someone else's console without consent;
- any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a community space.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban, temporarily or permanently, anyone whose behavior they consider harmful.

Report unacceptable behavior to studiosbeater@gmail.com. All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting is respected.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.
