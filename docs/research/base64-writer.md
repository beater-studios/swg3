# Base64 writer

ACE setup (by Mettrich) that writes a whole Pokémon from the box names read as Base64. Sources: `references/Gen3ACEPokemonBuilder` (MankeyMite, GPLv3; interactive guide at https://mankeymite.github.io/Gen3ACEPokemonBuilder/docs/spawn-setup-interactive.html) and `rawpk.txt` (public list of Pokémon with hex and names, by MankeyMite). Not run on this project's console.

## How it works

- Input: the 80 bytes of the box Pokémon in the game's format (encrypted), in Base64 with `+` replaced by `!` and `/` by `?`, without `=`. That is 107 characters: Boxes 1 to 13 with 8 and Box 14 with 3.
- The writer decodes and writes to Box 14, slot 30. It is data, not code: the code is the writer, installed once.
- Implemented in `src/swg3/gen3/base64box.py` (conversion and decoding) and in the "Put in your game" tab of the site.

## Setup on FR/LG

- ACE through species 0x0351 from the pre-E4 route ([ACE via box names](ace-box-names.md)).
- Five codes create a Metang named WELDR, which goes to Box 14, slot 28. A Pokémon with the nickname given in the guide goes to slot 29. There is an optional payload (DexReg) with a Switch version that registers the generated Pokémon in the Pokédex.
- Empty execution zone: from the 2nd row of Box 13 to Box 14, slot 30, except the writer's pieces. Ghost data in this zone also gets in the way.
- On the Switch, the setup codes are the same as on the cartridge; only DexReg changes (address of `UpdatePokedexForReceivedMon`, 0x08053F58 in English, checked against our `firered_switch` symbol table).

## Switch word filter

- The naming screen passes the text through `svc_BadWordCheck` (revision 10) and does not save what matches. The real list is not public.
- Model used: patterns from PKHeX's `badwords_switch.txt`, classified as "boundary" (blocks when standalone) and "strong" (blocks anywhere), with adjustments confirmed on a real Switch by the author of Gen3ACEPokemonBuilder (e.g. "xxx" with a digit in the middle). Implemented in `src/swg3/gen3/badwords.py`, data in `src/swg3/data/badwords_switch.txt` (generated by `tools/gen_badwords.py`).
- Workarounds for the Switch, without changing the bytes: cut the box before the last letter of the term and push the rest; replace an uppercase letter with an equivalent symbol (A `.`, B `-`, D `…`, E `“`, F `”`, G `‘`, H `’`, I `♂`, J `♀`, L `,`, N `/`), which the writer reads as the letter plus 64, compensating with the previous digit; pull the last letter of the previous box when a boundary term starts the box. If the console still refuses, convert all uppercase letters in the box.
- A shortened box cannot end with a space.

## Checking

- The site decodes the names pasted on the Codes page and shows the Pokémon that will come out.
- A typo in the encrypted area (from byte 32 on, Box 5 onward) breaks the checksum and becomes a Bad Egg. A typo in the header (PID, OT ID, nickname, OT, Boxes 1 to 4) passes the checksum and changes the Pokémon: compare the result shown.

## Verified with real data

- The 31 Pokémon in `rawpk.txt` decode with a correct checksum; our conversion reproduces the published names for 30 (the other has inconsistent hex and names in the text itself, in the OT area).
- Test vectors from Gen3ACEPokemonBuilder reproduced in `tests/test_base64box.py`.
